ASIO support in jdance

jdance hosts third-party ASIO drivers through its own adapter
(crates/audio-asio). The driver interface and data structures in that
adapter were written from the ASIO SDK headers by Steinberg Media
Technologies GmbH, which Steinberg offers under the GNU General Public
License version 3 as one of the SDK's licence options, and were checked
against the asio-sys 0.4.0 bindings (Apache-2.0). No ASIO SDK files are
distributed with jdance.
